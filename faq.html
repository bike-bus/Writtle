🚲 Writtle Bike Bus - Frequently Asked Questions

A community-led group ride to Writtle Junior School every Friday morning.

Writtle Bike Bus is a simple, friendly and supervised way for children and parents to cycle to school together. The ride follows one planned route through Writtle and is supported by adult marshals.

FAQ

What is Writtle Bike Bus?

Writtle Bike Bus is a community-led group cycle ride to Writtle Junior School. It is organised by local parents and volunteers to help children travel to school in a safer, more visible and more social way.

Is this run by the school?

No. This is a parent/community-led initiative. We aim to work positively with the school and local community, but the bike bus is organised separately by volunteers.

When does it run?

The bike bus runs every Friday morning, starting at 8:10am from Writtle Green Duck Pond.

Can children join along the route?

Yes. Riders can join at the Duck Pond or at designated pickup points along the route, as shown on the map. Please do not join unexpectedly at unsafe locations.

How many marshals are there?

We aim to have at least three adult marshals on every ride. If there are not enough marshals, the ride may be changed, delayed or cancelled.

Why do marshals use video cameras?

Some marshal bikes may use front and/or rear video cameras for safety, safeguarding and incident documentation. The purpose is not to film individuals unnecessarily, but to help record the ride environment if an incident or concern occurs.

Do children need helmets?

Yes. Children must wear a suitable cycling helmet. High-vis clothing, lights and a bike lock are also recommended.

Will the bike bus hold up traffic?

The aim is to ride calmly, predictably and courteously. A group of children cycling together may be more visible and easier to pass safely than lots of separate riders. Marshals will encourage the group to ride responsibly and follow the Highway Code.

What if I am a local resident or driver with a concern?

We welcome polite and constructive feedback. Please use the Contact / Report form so we can review concerns properly and improve how the bike bus operates.

Can my child ride without me?

This depends on the child’s age, confidence and the parent/carer’s judgement. Parents and carers remain responsible for deciding whether their child is suitable to participate and for completing the registration and waiver form.

What happens in bad weather?

The ride may be cancelled or changed if conditions are unsafe, for example in heavy rain, ice, poor visibility or high winds.

How do I volunteer?

Please use the volunteer/contact link above. We welcome help from parents and local community members who want to support safer, more active school travel.

🚲 Writtle Bike Bus

A community-led group ride to school every Friday morning.

Writtle Bike Bus is a simple, friendly and supervised way for children and parents to cycle to school together. The ride follows one planned route through Writtle and is supported by adult marshals.

Next ride: Every Friday
Start time: 8:10am
Start point: Writtle Green Duck Pond

How it works

  • Meet at Writtle Green Duck Pond before 8:10am.
  • The group rides together along one agreed route to school.
  • There will be at least three adult marshals on each ride.
  • Marshals help manage the group, support safe road positioning and assist at junctions.
  • Marshal bikes may carry front/rear video cameras for safety and incident recording.

Route

We currently operate as a single-route bike bus, starting from Writtle Green Duck Pond and riding together to school.

The route is designed to be simple, visible and manageable, rather than having multiple separate groups.

Safety requirements

To keep the ride safe and manageable, all riders should follow the basic requirements below:

  • Children must wear a suitable cycling helmet.
  • High-vis clothing or a bright jacket is strongly recommended.
  • Bikes must be roadworthy, with working brakes.
  • Children should be able to ride confidently in a group at a steady pace.
  • Younger children should ride with a parent or responsible adult.
  • Riders must follow marshal instructions during the ride.
  • No racing, weaving or overtaking within the group.

Registration and waiver

Before joining the ride, parents or carers should complete the registration and waiver form.

This helps us understand who is riding, collect emergency contact details and confirm that parents/carers understand the nature of the ride.

Volunteer as a marshal

The bike bus depends on local parent and community volunteers. We need enough adults to keep the ride calm, visible and well managed.

Marshal roles may include leading the group, riding at the back, helping at junctions or supporting younger riders.

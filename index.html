🚲 Writtle Bike Bus

A community-led group ride to Writtle Junior School every Friday morning.

Writtle Bike Bus is a simple, friendly and supervised way for children and parents to cycle to school together. The ride follows one planned route through Writtle and is supported by adult marshals.

Next ride: Every Friday
Start time: 8:10am
Main start point: Writtle Green Duck Pond

How it works

  • The main group starts at Writtle Green Duck Pond.
  • Riders can either meet at the Duck Pond or join along the route at designated pickup points shown on the route map.
  • The group rides together along one agreed route to Writtle Junior School.
  • There will be at least three adult marshals on each ride.
  • Marshals help manage the group, support safe road positioning and assist at junctions.
  • Marshal bikes may carry front/rear video cameras for safety, safeguarding and incident recording purposes.

Route

We currently operate as a single-route bike bus, starting from Writtle Green Duck Pond and riding together to school.

Riders may join at designated pickup points along the route where safe to do so.

Safety requirements

To help keep the ride safe, calm and manageable, all riders should follow the guidance below:

  • Children must wear a suitable cycling helmet.
  • High-vis clothing or a bright jacket is strongly recommended.
  • Bikes must be roadworthy, with working brakes and suitable tyres.
  • Front and rear bike lights are recommended, particularly during darker mornings.
  • A basic bike lock is recommended for bikes left at school.
  • Children should be able to ride confidently in a group at a steady pace.
  • Younger children should ride with a parent or responsible adult.
  • Riders must follow marshal instructions during the ride.
  • No racing, weaving or overtaking within the group.

Registration and waiver

Before joining the ride, parents or carers should complete the registration and waiver form.

This helps us understand who is riding, collect emergency contact details and confirm that parents/carers understand the nature of the ride.

The registration process will also include acknowledgement that marshal bikes may use video recording devices during rides for safety, safeguarding and incident documentation purposes.

Volunteer as a marshal

The bike bus depends on local parent and community volunteers. We need enough adults to help keep the ride visible, calm and well managed.

Marshal roles may include leading the group, riding at the rear, helping at junctions or supporting younger riders.
